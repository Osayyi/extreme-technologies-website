About Extreme Technologies

We put businesses on the map — from local visibility to global digital presence.

Your Complete Digital Growth Partner

Extreme Technologies was founded in 2025 with a clear mission: to help local businesses in Nigeria become accurately discoverable on Google Maps and Search. We started by verifying listings, correcting locations, and getting businesses "on the map" — often for the very first time.

What began as a focused effort to solve map visibility problems has quickly grown into a full digital agency. Today, we offer complete website design, development, Google Visibility, SEO, social media management, and paid advertising services. We handle the digital side of your business so you can focus on running it.
